OUR MISSION

Intelligence should be owned, not rented.

Sui juris: in one's own right, under no one else's authority.

The models are open and the GPUs are already on people's desks. We build the software between them, so that useful intelligence runs on hardware you own, answers to you, and keeps working with no signal, no account and nothing to rent.

THE TEAM

Who we are.

Who we are

Three engineers who kept running into the same wall: the models were open, the GPUs were on our desks, and the software between them treated our machines as a lesser case. We started with a tuner, found the engine underneath it wanting, and wrote our own.

We work in the open, in small measured steps, and we would rather publish a loss than a story.

HOW WE WORK

Six rules.

Kept in the repository and applied to every change, by people and by agents alike.
